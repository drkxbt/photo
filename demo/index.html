photo

A photo editor, with no dependencies

Crop, turn, flip and straighten. Adjust the light and colour or pick a look. Draw, point, box, write, and hide what should not be seen. Everything can be undone, and the finished picture comes back as a file. Free and MIT licensed, for the jobs people pay a yearly licence for.
